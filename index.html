At Crema Lounge, we believe that great food and coffee bring people together. Located at the Lower Ground of Foliage Mall in Islamabad's bustling F-7 Markaz, our lounge is much more than a typical cafe—it's a vibrant social hub that brings comfort, community, and culinary indulgence under one roof.

Whether you are catching up with friends over our beloved signature Spanish Latte, challenging each other to lively board games, indulging in authentic handcrafted cheesecakes, or enjoying late-night gourmet steaks, our kind and welcoming staff is here for you 24 hours a day.
